03 / Working principles

Make the work inspectable.

The goal is not just to show an outcome. Research and prototypes should make their assumptions, intermediate states, limitations, and reproduction paths accessible to others.

01

Explicit assumptions

Separate conceptual analogies, numerical observations, and empirically tested claims.

02

Reproducible artifacts

Publish runnable code, parameters, figures, and steps for verification where available.

03

Observable agent state

Prioritize traceable transitions, persistent context, and human approval boundaries.
